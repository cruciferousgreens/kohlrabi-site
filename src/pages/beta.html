The app is in beta.

Kohlrabi works, and people train with it. But expect a few rough edges, and tell us when you find them.

01

Things will break

New builds ship often — see what's new. If something looks wrong, try a refresh first, then report it below so it gets fixed for everyone.

02

Your data stays yours

Training data lives on your device first, and you can export it anytime as JSON. Beta or not, your log is never held hostage.

03

Reports shape the roadmap

Real bug reports from training get priority. The more specific (device, steps, what you expected), the faster it gets fixed.

Report a bug

Fill this in and we'll open a pre-filled issue on GitHub — you just hit submit there. You'll need a free GitHub account. Prefer to write it yourself? Open a blank issue →

The form opens github.com with your report filled in — nothing is sent until you submit it there.

No GitHub account? Send your bug report by email instead — same details, no account needed.

Report a bug by email

Include what happened, what you expected, and your device and app version — the more specific, the faster it gets fixed.

Keep it free.

Tracking and resolving bugs is hard work. This app is not my job, but your support helps me prioritize fixing what’s broken.
