Know the next jump.

When a lift comes in easy, Kohlrabi suggests the next weight, labeled with exactly what it's based on: your last session, your RPE, your rules. Tap to apply, or ignore it. Nothing changes unless you say so.

Top set 5 @ RPE 7, your +5 lb rule

Top set 5 @ RPE 8, reps climb before the load moves

30s hold @ RPE 8, your +15s time step

  • Only real history. No suggestions for lifts you haven't logged.
  • Labeled basis. Every suggestion says which rule and session it came from.
  • Your rules. Fixed pounds or percent, rep ranges, per-exercise overrides, deloads.
