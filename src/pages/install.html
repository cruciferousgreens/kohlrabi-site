Install Kohlrabi as a web app.

Currently Kohlrabi exists fully in browser. But you can still add it to your phone Home Screen and use it just like a regular app! This page shows you how.

  1. Open kohlrabi.us in Safari.

  2. Tap the Share button — the square with an arrow pointing up — in the toolbar.

  3. Scroll down in the share sheet and tap Add to Home Screen.

  4. Make sure Open as Web App is switched on, then tap Add in the top-right corner.

  5. You’ll find the Kohlrabi icon on your Home Screen — tap it and the app opens full-screen, just like a regular app.

  1. Open kohlrabi.us in Chrome.

  2. Tap the three-dot menu in the top-right corner.

  3. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen).

  4. Tap Install / Add to confirm.

  5. You’ll find the Kohlrabi icon on your home screen — tap it and the app opens full-screen, just like a regular app.
