Why Kohlrabi.

It's a vegetable. A weird-looking one.

Kohlrabi is a cruciferous vegetable — the same family as broccoli and kale. It grows a swollen stem that looks a little ridiculous, tastes mild and honest, and does its job without any fuss. That's about as close to a training philosophy as a name gets.

Getting stronger works the same way. No tricks, no 30-day transformations. You show up, add a little weight or a rep, and come back next week. Unglamorous, steady, and it works.

So the app got the vegetable's name. Kohlrabi is a free workout tracker by Cruciferous Greens — log your training, follow real progression, and keep the data yours.
