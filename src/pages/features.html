Set it. Share it.

Build the workout once: every exercise, set, and rep scheme. Send one link, and whoever opens it gets the whole thing: the muscle map, the exercise list, and a Start button that saves it straight into their library.

Programs share the same way. The full block, one link. No retyping from a screenshot. The workout arrives exactly as you built it.

Built for trainers too.

Write the program once and send your clients a link. They open it, save it to their library, and train it. No client accounts to manage, no per-seat fee. Yes, your clients use it free.

If the app earns its keep in your coaching business, consider contributing.

It tells you what to lift next.

Set a progression rule once: RPE-based double progression, straight linear jumps, or %1RM waves. When an exercise has real history behind it, the app turns the rule into a concrete suggestion. Tap the card to apply it.

No history, no guesswork. Suggestions only ever come from your own logged sets, and each one is labeled with exactly what it's based on.

Progression paths.

Double progression, straight linear jumps, percentages of your max, or plain reps. Pick a path per program and the app turns your logged history into concrete suggestion cards. Flip through the paths, then tap a card to try it.

See what you've actually trained.

Each exercise maps its primary and secondary muscles onto an anatomical front-and-back chart. At a glance you know what's getting work and what's been quiet for three weeks.

The same maps roll up across programs, so a whole training block's coverage is visible before you run it.

Dumbbells without the mental math.

Log the weight in each hand. The number printed on the dumbbell. Volume counts both sides automatically, and a per-exercise toggle switches to combined weight if you prefer it.

Projected 1RM, heaviest-set PRs, and per-exercise notes ride along on every exercise detail page. Pounds or kilograms, volume or sets. Your defaults live in Settings.

Warm up without thinking.

Say how many warm-up sets you want and the app builds the ladder from your working weight. The sets get tagged warmup and count toward your volume like everything else. They're never added automatically.

Programs that fit your week.

Build a program out of your saved workouts, or start from a proven template like 5/3/1 or an Arnold split. There's no enforced order and no guilt for skipped days: train the sessions in whatever sequence your week allows.

Your training history, kept.

Every finished workout becomes a log: sets, notes, and PRs, filed by date. A year from now you can see exactly what you did, and what it led to.

Open any exercise for its full story: projected max, heaviest sets, personal records, and the muscles it trains.

Just lift. Or dig in the weeds.

The defaults stay out of your way. When you want control, it's there: units, dumbbell weight mode, progression rules per exercise, warm-up ladders, deload scheduling.

Set it once and forget it, or tune every detail. The app doesn't care which.

What it doesn't have.

This is an app truly not trying to sell you on anything. Because of that, here's a list of stuff it doesn't have that other apps do:

  • exercise photos/videos
  • rest timers
  • plate calculators

Other stuff it's got.

Cloud syncing

Sign in and your log follows you. Phone, tablet, desktop: same workouts, same history, nothing to transfer by hand.

Exports

Your entire history as JSON, one tap. Back it up, move it, open it in a spreadsheet. It's yours.

Exercise library

A real exercise database with muscles worked for every movement. Missing something? Add your own custom exercises.

Works offline

Local-first. Log in a basement gym with no signal; everything is there when you're back. No account wall to start.

Private by design

No ads, no data sale, no engagement tricks. Your training log is yours and stays that way.
