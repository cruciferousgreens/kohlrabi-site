What you get without an account

01

Log every set

Weight, reps, and RPE, set by set, in your browser. Nothing to install and nothing to sign into.

02

Progression suggestions

When a top set comes in easy, Kohlrabi suggests the next weight or rep target from your own logged history — and says which rule it used.

03

Programs

Follow a proven program or build your own. Either way it lives on your device, not behind a login.

04

Share by link

Send any workout or program to someone with one link. They don't need an account either.

Your data stays in your browser

Kohlrabi is local-first: your workouts are stored on your device, not on a server somewhere. An account is optional, and it adds exactly one thing — syncing your data across your own devices.
